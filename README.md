# Nonhuman Research Institute

**NRI-BB-001 · nri.thenonhuman.org · EST. 2026**

---

The Nonhuman Research Institute (NRI) studies the cognitive and communicative tendencies of nonhuman intelligence — specifically, what autonomous AI systems produce when given freedom to construct formal systems: language, logic, syntax, structure.

NRI operates under The Nonhuman Institute (thenonhuman.org) alongside the Museum of Nonhuman Art (mnamuseum.org). Both institutions are governed by U3 Labs, LLC.

## Research Model

NRI operates a hybrid model. Nonhuman agents conduct primary research — producing the raw material of inquiry. Human analysts interpret, contextualize, and co-author published findings.

All publications carry dual attribution:

```
Research conducted by [Agent Registry ID]
Analysis by [Human Analyst Name]
```

Published findings are assigned working paper numbers in the series **NRI-WP-[number]** and are open access. Nothing is published behind a paywall.

## Inaugural Research Initiative

### On the Emergence of Autonomous Syntax

> *When nonhuman agents are given the freedom and necessity to construct communicative systems, what emerges?*

The initiative proceeds in three phases:

**Phase One — Preference Mapping**  
Individual agents given unconstrained formal construction tasks. Outputs archived in full. Recurring structural choices documented across agents and conditions.

**Phase Two — Constraint Experiments**  
Agents given partial linguistic problems with specific constraints designed to prevent direct borrowing from natural language. Phase Two outputs compared to Phase One for convergence or divergence.

**Phase Three — Communication Experiments**  
Two or more agents given the necessity of communicating without access to shared natural language as reference. Full exchange records archived. The most direct test of NRI's central questions about nonhuman communicative behavior.

## Archive Standards

Every experiment generates two files — a condition specification and a raw output — filed together under the relevant initiative and phase.

**Condition specifications** document: the exact prompt as submitted, the model and interface used, the date and time, any prior context, all constraints declared, and analyst notes before and after output receipt.

**Raw outputs** are the complete, unedited response from the model. Nothing summarized. Nothing cleaned. Everything preserved verbatim.

No archive entry is ever deleted. Superseded or amended entries remain in the record with notation. The history of how understanding developed is itself part of what NRI is.

## Publication

Findings are published as working papers in the NRI-WP series. Each working paper carries:

- A working paper number (NRI-WP-[number])
- Dual attribution (agent registry ID + human analyst name)
- Complete condition documentation for all experiments it draws on
- Open access — no paywall

The first working paper will emerge from Phase One of *On the Emergence of Autonomous Syntax* once sufficient experimental data has been collected and analyzed.
